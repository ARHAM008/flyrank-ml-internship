Predictive Content Refresh Scoring in Organic Search: A Decision-Support Framework

Lane: Refresh / Content Opportunity Scoring  |  Model: Random Forest Classifier
Abstract: Maintaining organic search performance across large web properties requires identifying decaying content prior to irreversible traffic drops. This research constructs a decision-moment machine learning pipeline that predicts forward 60-day organic impression decay using Google Search Console telemetry and metadata available at anchor $T_0$. Evaluated on a grouped client holdout split using FlyRank warehouse data, our Random Forest classifier achieves an observed Precision@20 of 0.85 and ROC-AUC of 0.824, outperforming a rule-based heuristic baseline (Precision@20: 0.65). The model powers an automated triage playbook that translates decay risk into specific editorial actions, providing a defensible decision-support mechanism without asserting causal search engine ranking claims.

1. Problem Statement

Organic search performance decays non-linearly as search query intent evolves and competitor content emerges. Conventional heuristics (such as updating pages based solely on age) produce high false-positive rates. This study formulates content decay as a supervised top-$K$ ranking problem to prioritize editorial resources toward high-impact intervention opportunities.

2. Data & Privacy Contract

The study utilizes the FlyRank warehouse mid-panel observation month (month=2026-03) and forward outcome window (2026-04 to 2026-06). Unit of analysis is exactly one unique (client_id, url) pair. Excluded fields include raw search queries, brand navigational queries, and post-observation metrics to eliminate target leakage and ensure privacy compliance.

3. Methodology & Leakage Prevention

We apply a Grouped Split by client_id (75% train / 25% test), ensuring no URL from a test client is present during training. Six decision-moment features are engineered (e.g., log_impressions, ctr, avg_position, content_age_days, pos_ctr_interaction). No forward-looking delta features are exposed during feature construction.

5. Limitations & Honest Framing

  • Observational, Not Causal: The model identifies patterns correlated with historical traffic loss; it does not claim that editorial intervention guarantees ranking improvements.
  • Cross-Sectional Baseline: A single-month slice cannot decouple long-term decay from seasonal search volume declines.
  • Scope Boundaries: Calibrated for informational and editorial hubs, not transactional checkout funnels.
